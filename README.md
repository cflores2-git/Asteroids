# Asteroids

**[Play in your browser on Unity Play](https://play.unity.com/en/games/908d04e5-74f0-4df0-98ec-3ac497edaea1/asteroids)**

Asteroids is a Unity and C# arcade shooter. Rotate and thrust to steer your ship, dodge incoming asteroids, and shoot them to increase your score.

## Gameplay systems

- Ship rotation, forward thrust, projectile firing, and screen wraparound.
- Asteroid spawning and movement.
- Score tracking and a difficulty increase once the player reaches a score threshold.

## Code to explore

- [PlayerMovement.cs](Assets/Scripts/PlayerMovement.cs) — ship controls, firing, and screen boundaries.
- [AsteroidSpawner.cs](Assets/Scripts/AsteroidSpawner.cs) — asteroid spawning.
- [GameManager.cs](Assets/Scripts/GameManager.cs) — scoring and difficulty.
